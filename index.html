Workouts

Workouts with a Google Health export get the full heart-rate graphic — the continuous curve colored by zone, exactly as in the Google Health app. Workouts without an export sit on that day's hourly-average curve with the session window shaded (Health Connect syncs only hourly averages to Muse). Per-workout energy from Health Connect is not shown — it reports implausibly low values (median 11 kcal); energy appears only when it comes from a Google Health export.
